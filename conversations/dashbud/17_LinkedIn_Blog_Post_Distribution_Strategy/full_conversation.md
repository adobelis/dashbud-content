 Based on your blog post, here are key considerations for sharing it on LinkedIn from your company page:

## Content Strategy Considerations

**Positioning & Tone**
- Your blog post is thoughtful and industry-focused rather than overtly promotional, which works well for LinkedIn's professional audience
- The problem-solution narrative you've established resonates with the pain points many LinkedIn users (especially in data/analytics roles) experience daily
- Consider this as thought leadership content that establishes Dashbud's expertise in the BI space

**Target Audience Alignment**
- LinkedIn's professional audience likely includes many people who've lived through the BI frustrations you describe
- Your content speaks directly to executives, data analysts, IT leaders, and business users - all well-represented on LinkedIn

## Recommended Approach

**Post Type: Native LinkedIn Article vs. Link Share**
I'd recommend creating a **native LinkedIn post with key excerpts** rather than just sharing the link. This typically gets better engagement than external link shares.

**Structure & Length**
- **Hook (1-2 sentences)**: Lead with a relatable pain point
- **Key insight (2-3 sentences)**: Your main thesis about why BI hasn't delivered
- **Brief solution preview (1-2 sentences)**: Hint at what's possible with AI
- **Call to action**: Invite discussion + link to full post
- **Total length**: 150-200 words max

**Sample Opening Hook Options:**
- "After 20 years of BI promises, why are sales managers still rebuilding the same Excel analysis every month?"
- "We invested in dashboards, hired analysts, centralized our data... so why is everyone still waiting weeks for answers?"

**Engagement Strategy**
- Ask a question to encourage comments ("What's the biggest reporting bottleneck in your organization?")
- Tag relevant industry connections or partners who might amplify
- Consider timing for maximum visibility (Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 3-5 PM)

**Follow-up Considerations**
- Be prepared to engage actively in comments - this type of thought leadership post often generates discussion
- Consider creating a series if this performs well, diving deeper into specific pain points you mentioned

The blog post itself is well-positioned for LinkedIn sharing - it's problem-aware, solution-oriented, and speaks to real professional challenges without being overly sales-focused.
