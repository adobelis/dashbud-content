# **Dashbud LinkedIn Outreach Drafts**

## **Bestiary of cold email structures**

Six classic structures, each sketched for Dashbud. Same offer, different emphasis.

### PAS: Problem, Agitate, Solution 
**Present a problem; "agitate" by suggesting how it might affect them; present our solution**
Problem: Everyone wants AI answers from company data. 
Agitate: When each person runs their own ChatGPT workflow, you get five answers to one question and sensitive data in the wrong places. 
Solution: Dashbud, one governed source with AI on top. 

### BAB: Before, After, Bridge 
**Transition a reader from a current pain point to a desired solution.**
Before: Tell me if I'm off-base: today, your reporting is blocked. A many-headed hydra stands between your company and great data visibility: incomplete data-integration projects; overworked analysts; shared dashboards that aren't quite flexible enough; security and governance worries, not the least being with AI itself. 
After: Imagine a world where your company has access to the data they need: self-service, but with shared, governed resources. Shareable dashboards, yes, but also the ability to ask for custom reportsin plain English and get the right answer in seconds. 
Bridge: This, in short, is what Dashbud offers. And you can be live in days, without a six-month, six-figure project. Let me know if you'd like to see a demo, or click the link to find out more.

### AIDA: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action 
Attention: Data is born free, and yet everywhere it is in chains. BI tools promised to free it, to make your whole company data-aware. For most of us, the promise has not been met fully: we have analysts and dashboards, but a lot of difficult gaps to fill.
Interest:  AI promises to take us the rest of the way. But which AI? LLM-based workflows create a mess of vibe-code producing unaudited calculations, while exposing your data to third-party AI. Integrators offer you to help you, but the overhead and project commitment is daunting
Desire: Dashbud fills in the gap: a flexible, self-service platform that integrates data resources from databases and APIs to flat files and delivers shareable reporting, correct answers, and secure data governance. 
Action: Worth a 10-minute look? 

###  OPPA: Observation, Problem, Proof, Ask 
**Something specific about them, the likely problem, evidence, a small ask. Probably better once our proof is deeper but could work for an ERP-based prospect**
Observation: I saw you joined [company] as COO after the acquisition.
Problem: New owners need great data visibility without margin-eating projects. 
Proof: With Dashbud, governed, secure reporting is at your fingertips. We connected a ~$50 million distributor with a legacy ERP in days. 
Ask: Worth a quick call? 

### Question-led 
**Flattering question, the "without" list, the answer**
Who: As someone running operations, you've probably asked: 
Basic question: How can AI improve my company's data visibility 
Complication: without exposing sensitive data, turning staff into vibe coders, producing code nobody can support?
Answer: Dashbud.

### Star, Story, Solution
**A hero like them, their story, how it ended - use once Omega has a winning story to tell**
A PE-backed operator buys a family-run manufacturer, needs visibility on day one, can't spend ~$100K on a data project. Dashbud gets them reporting in days.

## Prompting guide: one message per prospect

Use this with a model (e.g. Sonnet) and a list of leads. For each lead, pick one draft above as the base and adapt it lightly using the hooks below.

### Input per lead

Name, title, company, industry, rough headcount, signals (new in role, recent post topic, ERP or systems keywords), any mutual connection.

### **Role hooks**

| Role | What they care about | Lead with |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| COO / VP Operations | Visibility across sites, inventory, speed of answers | Drafts 4, 5, 7, 8 |
| CFO / Finance lead | One set of numbers, margins, cost control, governance | Draft 9, Draft 1 |
| CEO / Owner / PE operator | Value of the business, margins, no big projects | Draft 7, margin angle |
| CTO / CIO | Governance, security, API, no shadow AI | Drafts 1, 2 |

### **Industry hooks**

* Distribution: warehouses, slow stock, margin by product line.  
* Manufacturing: production costs, scrap, on-time delivery, legacy ERP.  
* Healthcare clinics: patient data never goes to the AI, multi-office performance (Draft 3).

### **Signal hooks**

* New in role: speak to "leaders retooling their team" in general terms. Never say "I noticed you just started."  
* Recent post: one light nod to the topic only if it relates naturally.  
* Legacy ERP: use the "your ERP has run the business for twenty years" angle without naming the system.  
* Mutual connection: mention them by name in the first line.

### **Rules**

**Every message, every role: raise governance, security and shared definitions.** These are the two differentiators. Plant the question even if it isn't on their mind: using AI on company data without governance means real risk, and without shared definitions everyone gets a different answer. The answer is a tool with both baked in.

* Under 90 words, plain language, one question as the ask.  
* Specific but not creepy: describe what people in their position usually face.  
* Flatter the reader as smart and forward-thinking.  
* Don't open with "Imagine."  
* Honest proof only: "We're already live in mid-market distribution." Never imply multiple customers or name Omega or its systems.  
* Save the Star, Story, Solution structure until there's a customer win.
