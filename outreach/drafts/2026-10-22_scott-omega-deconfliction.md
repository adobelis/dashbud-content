## Why this framing

- **Deconfliction, not permission.** Frames it as coordination between partners rather than asking Scott to approve Arthur's access to a customer.
- **Leads with the shared risk.** The renewal clock is a Dashbud problem with a date on it — concrete, not a turf argument.
- **Explicit lane boundary.** Removes the obvious objection (Arthur freelancing into a live negotiation) before Scott can raise it.
- **Ends on Scott's self-interest.** Adoption makes his custom-work proposal easier to sell. True, and it reframes the ask as help rather than competition.
- **No criticism of Scott's approach.** The transparency and Evolytix/Dashbud blending concerns are real but belong in the broader pipeline-ownership conversation, not in a single-account message. Raising them here would make this about Scott instead of about Omega.

## If Scott declines or slow-walks
