## How they found us

## How the demo went

### The pitch (pre-demo)
Arthur positioned Dashbud against the "just use Claude/AI" approach:
- Why you shouldn't vibe-code your analytics: non-deterministic results, sustainability risk if someone leaves, privacy/HIPAA concerns
- Dashbud as a structured system vs. ad-hoc AI automation
- Privacy-first: AI never sees raw data

Michelle's response: "I don't have any questions initially. I think we just need to dive more into what it looks like." She wanted to see the product, not hear the pitch.

### The demo
- Showed data import, semantic model setup, Data Explorer
- Walked through conversational querying (diagnoses by type, provider procedures, location comparisons)
- Michelle asked good questions:
  - Can we set up favorites for recurring monthly reports? (Yes — showed favorites and dashboards)
  - Can we compare year-over-year (this month 2026 vs 2025 vs 2024 by provider)? 
- Aaron asked about filtering by multiple dimensions (locations, diagnoses, insurance payments) — confirmed capable

### The stumble
- Year-over-year comparison query didn't work cleanly during the demo. Arthur had done this many times before (it's a basic Omega use case) but the agent fumbled it with this dataset. Recovered by offering a customized follow-up demo.
- Possible causes: agent drift, dataset not fully tuned for this query pattern, or just bad luck with non-deterministic behavior.

### Pricing discussion
- Aaron asked about pricing — a buying signal
- Arthur quoted: $40/mo annual, $50/mo monthly, per seat, 2-seat minimum
- Aaron: "So if Michelle and I just want access... it's $100/month?" — Yes
- Scott's postmortem reaction: "We're f***ing giving this thing away" — feels underpriced but right for market share at this stage

## Outcome
