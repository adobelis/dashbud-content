# Betaworks

NYC startup studio turned venture fund and incubator, founded 2008 by John Borthwick. Known for early bets (Tumblr, Kickstarter, Bitly, Giphy, Chartbeat) and for being Hugging Face's first investor (Hugging Face came out of the first Camp in 2016; Betaworks' Sept 2026 post covers the Hugging Face + Nvidia deal). Recent: Granola (unicorn, March 2026), The Browser Company, RecRoom.

**Fund 3.0:** $66M, announced July 2025. Pre-seed/seed. Thesis: founders "shaping the future with ML/AI," "zero-to-one product development within a specific theme."

**Camp:** 12-week in-person program in their Meatpacking offices, about eight companies per cohort, two cohorts a year, each on a theme. Terms (Fall '26): up to $500K per company; Betaworks up to $250K on an uncapped SAFE with 25% discount plus 5% common equity; syndicate partners up to $250K more on matching terms. Camp director: Analisa Svehaug. Borthwick (Oct 2026 podcast): roughly 20 Camp notes a year at ~$500K, plus pre-seed checks around $750K for ~35% equity (reported; verify).

Camp themes, in order: Augment (summer '23), Agents (spring '24), Native Applications (fall '24), Application Layer (spring '25), Interfaces (fall '25), Agent Systems (spring '26, ran Mar 2 – May 15), The New Agentic Economy (fall '26, Sept 14 – Nov 20; demo day week of Nov 16; applications closed).

## Why they matter to Dashbud

Their writing is the closest thing to the Downstream thesis from an investor who has been in it since 2016. Borthwick on the app layer: "After years of compounding value at the foundation and infrastructure layers, it's thrilling to see the speed and pace of the app layer maturing." Jordan Crook's memory deep dive lands on the same conclusion as the Downstream map: the differentiator is the curator, "who or what decides what memories are stored, retrieved, and promoted." That is the skipped step, from a different direction.

They are also the local scene: the office is an event space, and the Fall '26 demo day is the week of Nov 16, 2026.

## Where Dashbud fits, and doesn't

Camp is for pre-seed founders at zero-to-one with large equity terms; Dashbud is past that stage and shouldn't apply. The fit is as a peer in the argument: cite their writing, engage their posts, show up to events, and keep them in view for a later round or for intros to portfolio companies that need governed data access (anything in the Agent Systems or Agentic Economy cohorts that touches business data).
