# Nicola Hallenborg ("Nikki")

GTM lead at Amazon Business, previously TikTok. Known through running.

**Not a prospect, and not a product evaluator.** She is a potential **GTM advisor** — someone who can advise on approach and execution. That is a different thing from a gatekeeper or user trying the product to judge fit (see `dani-schnackenberg.md` for that case).

## Why she's useful

A GTM lead at TikTok and Amazon has run this motion at scale. Worth treating as an advisor alongside Lovett, Rodgers, and Elser — her perspective is more recent and more operational than most.

**Her own framing: more strategic than tactical.** Fine — both levels are useful, and the strategic read may be more valuable right now.

Strategic questions worth putting to her:
- Is the manufacturing/distribution vertical choice right for round 1?
- Positioning: does the privacy and shared-definitions story differentiate, or is it table stakes by now?
- Sequencing: outbound now, or more warm-track and content first?
- Is the adoption problem (customers who pay and never log in) a GTM problem or a product problem?

**Not the person for Sales Navigator mechanics** — she has likely never used the tool. What she probably does have is experience **planning campaigns and evaluating execution**, which is the more useful layer anyway: is the campaign designed sensibly, are the benchmarks right (~200 accounts, 5 customers in 2-3 months, the <5% reply decision gate), and how would she judge whether it is working.

Ask her to critique the plan, not the tool.
