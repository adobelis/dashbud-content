# Carmelo Cruz Reyes

Strategy and growth consultant in healthcare, currently interim (likely fractional) Head of Growth at **Zocalo Health**. Co-founded **Yuvo Health** and rolled off that role a couple of years ago. Lists consulting continuously since 2016.

Met through **Patrick McGovern** at the Callen-Lorde Community Health Awards — first at last year's event, reconnected 2026-11-05. Two encounters a year apart is real rapport, not a cold event contact.

## How he's actually useful

Not a buyer — he is fractional and consulting, not running an organization with a data budget. Two other things though:

**Network into community health.** Yuvo Health works with FQHCs and community health centers: multi-site, regulated, chronically under-resourced on data, served badly by their EMRs. That is adjacent to the ICP, and the "AI never sees your raw data" architecture is the strongest opener in that world — the same angle that moved ADAPT.

**Growth perspective.** A healthcare growth consultant has sold into these organizations and knows how they buy, which is distinct from what Lovett, Rodgers, and Nikki can offer.

## The bigger idea: Carmelo as a channel, not just an introduction

Two levels of value here, and the second is larger.

**Introductions.** He knows community health organizations and can point at opportunities.

**Dashbud inside his methodology.** Growth consulting requires understanding a client's data fast. He cannot build custom infrastructure per engagement, and his clients have no data teams. Dashbud could be part of how he works — either he uses it to analyze client data, or he deploys it for them as part of the engagement. One relationship, many deployments.

### Worth designing deliberately, because we already run this model by accident

Scott and Evolytix are effectively a consultant channel for Dashbud already, and it is producing the problems documented across this pipeline: Dashbud priced against services rates rather than as software, pitched inside someone else's contracting cycle, with no visibility into the thread. See [[2026-10-22-omega-scott-deconfliction]].

So the model works — it is where most of the current pipeline came from. But if Carmelo becomes a channel, the terms should be explicit from the start: who owns the customer relationship, how Dashbud is priced within an engagement, whether he resells or refers, and what Dashbud's visibility into his accounts is.

Getting that right with Carmelo is also a template for fixing it with Scott.

## The fractional-exec pattern

Worth noticing: Omega's real stakeholder is a fractional CFO, and Carmelo is a fractional head of growth. Fractional executives get hired precisely by organizations that cannot staff a function full-time — the same profile as "no data team." They also serve several clients at once, so one who likes Dashbud is a multiplier rather than a single seat.

Possibly a channel worth thinking about on its own, separate from Carmelo.
