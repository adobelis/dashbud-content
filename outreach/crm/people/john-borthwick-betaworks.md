# John Borthwick

Founded Betaworks in 2008. Hugging Face's first investor. Writes on betaworks.com (less often since 2024; Jordan Crook now carries most of it), Medium (@Borthwick), and X (@Borthwick), where he posts Camp calls and reads applications publicly.

## What he thinks (Oct 2026, How I Invest podcast; earlier posts)

- "The cost of building a startup is trending towards zero," which makes the founder matter more, not less. Founders "are more often than not steering a set of technologies, a set of agents, a set of AI tools and other humans towards the goal."
- Agents will be the catch-all term for native AI software the way "apps" was for mobile; "agents are going to eat software alive."
- On the app layer: "After years of compounding value at the foundation and infrastructure layers, it's thrilling to see the speed and pace of the app layer maturing."
- AI risk: "overall bad, but not existential," compared to Y2K.
- Founder tell: he asks every founder to explain their company name; a weak answer is a red flag.

## Dashbud angle

Agrees with the Downstream thesis at the level of where value accrues; the disagreement, if any, is emphasis.
