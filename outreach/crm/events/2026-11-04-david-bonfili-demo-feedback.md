## The most valuable output: specification / generation / validation

**David's contribution, relaying Tymofiy Mylovanov** (president, Kyiv School of Economics; former Ukrainian economy minister), who uses the frame to integrate AI into curriculum. Unverified whether published.

> AI lets you outsource **generation** — the work you would have done manually. It does *not* let you outsource **specification** or **validation**. Those become materially *more* important: specify wrongly or validate poorly and you get garbage out, and don't know it.

His application, near-verbatim: *one way of thinking about what you're doing is providing pressure around specification and validation, that allows for more effective use of generation without putting that burden wholly on every individual user.*

**Why this beats the governance framing.** "Shared rules and governance" is a feature argument and invites a feature rebuttal. This is a claim about what AI categorically cannot do — collapsing the cost of generation *raises* the value of the stages either side. Dashbud stops competing with the model and starts supplying what the model can't: the semantic model is specification institutionalized; auditable SQL is validation institutionalized.

It also answers the breadth problem: the qualifier is not an industry, it is **organizations where more than one person has to agree what a number means**. And it bears on pricing — a reporting convenience is a $100/mo seat; the layer that makes everyone else's generation trustworthy is priced per organization.

**The thin leg, worth not overclaiming.** Specification is genuinely strong. Validation is weaker than the framing implies: auditable SQL makes a number *checkable by a human*, it does not check anything itself. Adopting this language invites "so how do you validate?" and the honest answer today is that we make validation possible, not automatic. A wrong definition still yields wrong numbers — consistently and visibly wrong, which is recoverable, rather than variably and silently wrong, which is not. Metric assertions and reconciliation checks are the roadmap opening.
