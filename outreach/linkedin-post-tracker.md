# LinkedIn Post Tracker

Running log of Arthur's LinkedIn posts: performance, promotion legwork, and what came of it.

**How to use this:** add a new `##` section per post. Update stats in place as they accumulate — LinkedIn posts keep earning impressions for 48–72 hours, and comments can trickle for a week. Timestamp each reading so trends stay readable.

**What this is not:** a virality scoreboard. Round 1 is about getting comfortable posting, showing LinkedIn this account has an engaged network, and putting out something genuine and well-crafted. Targeting comes later.

### Read on the numbers (first reading)

- **4.1% engagement rate** (40/965). Typical LinkedIn is 2–3%; strong for a first post from an account with no posting history in this space.
- **48% out-of-network** — the algorithm chose to show it beyond Arthur's connections. Notable with no posting history; implies the early engagement signal was good.
- **0.57 comment-to-reaction ratio** (13:23). Most posts run 0.1–0.2. People wrote something rather than just tapping like — the signal LinkedIn weights most heavily, and the likely cause of the out-of-network reach.
- **2 reposts** punch above their weight: each re-seeds the post into a new network.

**Caveat:** engagement from a network Arthur personally activated measures that network's goodwill, not market demand. That is the correct goal for a launch post — the warm track working as intended — but it is not ICP validation.

### Promotion legwork

Substantial manual effort, tracked by Arthur.

- **~30 of 50 planned outreaches** completed as of 10/22 (text and direct messages)
- **Escalation tactic:** hold high-value promoters until the post is ~1 day old. Showing them a post with visible traction is a better ask than a raw new post — it gives them social proof and makes a boost feel natural rather than like a favor. The post is deliberately non-time-sensitive so it holds up to the delay. **Worth reusing.**
