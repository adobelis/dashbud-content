## Why Thinking About Advance Matters Beyond Outreach
Advance is a useful lens for Dashbud's marketing and positioning work. Arthur engaged in substantive policy and vendor acquisition/management discussions there, dealing with all the decisionmaker personalities and their respective interests and objections. Mapping out what each person at Advance would get from Dashbud — and what their resistance would look like — is a proxy for the kinds of conversations that happen inside any mid-market org considering a new data tool:
- **The data owner** (Mark) who sees any new tool as a threat to his turf
- **The conservative ops leader** (Scott) who needs to see cost/efficiency, not innovation
- **The forward-thinking architect** (Mozam) who appreciates doing things the right way
- **The budget holder** (Matt J) who only cares if it reduces headcount or vendor spend
- **The data wonk** (Zoltan) who genuinely wants to see what the product can do
- **The cross-functional partner** (Kim) who bridges data and business but has political ties to the incumbent

These archetypes show up at every target company. The marketing copy should speak to multiple personas, not just one.
