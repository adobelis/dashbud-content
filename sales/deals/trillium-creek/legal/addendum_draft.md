## Additional Sections to Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement

The following sections are added to the Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement between HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc. and Arthur Dobelis and Scott Binger, a general partnership trading as Dashbud.

### 17. Mutual Confidentiality

This NDA applies reciprocally. All references to "Recipient" shall apply to either Party when receiving Confidential Information from the other. All references to "HMT" as the disclosing Party shall apply equally to Dashbud when Dashbud is the disclosing Party.

### 18. Limited Publicity Consent

Notwithstanding Section 11 of the NDA, upon HMT's first payment to Dashbud under a paid subscription or service agreement, HMT consents to Dashbud identifying HMT as a Dashbud customer in marketing materials, customer lists, and website content. HMT may revoke this consent upon thirty (30) days' written notice, after which Dashbud shall remove HMT's name from future materials within a commercially reasonable time.

### 19. Entity Assignment

The Parties acknowledge that Dashbud is currently a general partnership. Upon formation of a successor corporate entity that will carry on Dashbud's business, Dashbud may assign the NDA to the successor entity without HMT's prior consent, provided that the successor entity assumes all obligations under the NDA in a writing delivered to HMT. Dashbud shall notify HMT of the assignment within thirty (30) business days, including the successor entity's legal name, state of formation, and principal address.
