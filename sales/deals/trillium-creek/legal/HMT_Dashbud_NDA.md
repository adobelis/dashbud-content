NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT
HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc. and Dashbud
This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of __________________, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("HMT"), and Dashbud ______________________________, a ______________________________ ("Recipient"). HMT and Recipient may each be a "Party" and collectively the "Parties."
1. Purpose
HMT is evaluating and/or implementing Recipient’s software, technology, analytics, reporting, artificial intelligence, automation, or related services (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, HMT may provide Recipient access to confidential, proprietary, financial, operational, scheduling, appointment, business, employee, provider, patient, billing, reimbursement, and other information. Recipient shall use that information solely for the Purpose and protect it under this Agreement.
2. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed or made available to Recipient by or for HMT, whether electronically, orally, visually, in writing, through system access, data exports, or otherwise. It includes, without limitation:
Financial information, including revenue, charges, payments, collections, accounts receivable, reimbursement, payer information, fee schedules, financial reports, and performance data;
Billing, claims, patient appointment, scheduling, utilization, encounter, demographic, and other patient-related information;
Protected Health Information ("PHI") and personally identifiable information ("PII"), to the extent lawfully disclosed;
Provider productivity, compensation-related, scheduling, utilization, and performance information; employee and staffing information;
Business plans, workflows, policies, strategies, pricing, contracts, vendor information, reports, spreadsheets, databases, exports, dashboards, analyses, credentials, system configurations, and security information;
Information derived from, incorporating, summarizing, analyzing, modeling, or otherwise reflecting HMT information; and
Any information a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.
Confidential Information is protected whether or not marked "Confidential."
3. Recipient Obligations
Recipient shall:
Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose and disclose it only to personnel, contractors, agents, or professional advisers who have a legitimate need to know and are bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
Remain responsible for violations by persons to whom it provides access;
Apply reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, and at least the same care it uses for its own similarly sensitive information;
Limit access, copying, retention, downloading, reproduction, and transfer to the minimum reasonably necessary for the Purpose;
Not sell, rent, license, commercialize, disclose, or independently exploit HMT information, or use it to develop products or services for third parties without HMT’s written permission;
Not identify or re-identify individuals from de-identified, aggregated, or anonymized information;
Comply with applicable privacy and data-security laws; and
Promptly notify HMT of unauthorized access, acquisition, use, disclosure, loss, or compromise.
4. Artificial Intelligence and Model Training
Unless HMT expressly authorizes it in a separate signed writing, Recipient shall not use HMT Confidential Information, patient information, PHI, PII, or data derived from them to train, retrain, fine-tune, validate, improve, or develop any artificial intelligence model, machine-learning model, large language model, algorithm, generalized dataset, or product for Recipient or any third party. HMT data shall not be included in shared, public, multi-customer, or generally available training datasets. Any processing must be limited to providing services specifically for HMT.
5. Ownership
All Confidential Information remains the property of HMT or its applicable owner. No ownership, license, intellectual property, or other right transfers to Recipient except the limited right to use the information for the Purpose. Processing, analyzing, organizing, transforming, or storing HMT data does not give Recipient ownership of it.
6. HIPAA and PHI
This Agreement does not replace a Business Associate Agreement ("BAA"). If Recipient creates, receives, maintains, transmits, accesses, or otherwise processes PHI on HMT’s behalf as a HIPAA Business Associate, the Parties shall execute a separate BAA before PHI is disclosed or access is provided. Recipient shall not access or use PHI before all legally required agreements are in effect.
7. Incident Notice
Recipient shall notify HMT without unreasonable delay and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery of any actual or reasonably suspected unauthorized access, acquisition, use, disclosure, loss, theft, or system compromise involving Confidential Information. Recipient shall fully cooperate in investigation, mitigation, documentation, and response. A shorter period in law, the BAA, or another signed agreement controls.
8. Exclusions
Confidential Information excludes information Recipient demonstrates through contemporaneous written records: (a) was lawfully known without restriction before HMT disclosed it; (b) became public through no breach by Recipient; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality duty; or (d) was independently developed without use of or reference to HMT information.
9. Required Disclosure
If legally compelled to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall, to the extent permitted, promptly notify HMT, allow a reasonable opportunity to seek protection, cooperate with HMT, and disclose only the minimum legally required.
10. Return and Destruction
At HMT’s written request or when the relationship or evaluation ends, Recipient shall cease use and, at HMT’s direction, return or securely destroy Confidential Information, including copies, extracts, downloads, and derivative datasets, except as legally required. Recipient shall certify completion on request. Information in routine backups remains protected, may be used only for disaster recovery, and shall be deleted through ordinary secure retention procedures.
11. No Publicity
Recipient shall not use HMT’s name, trademarks, logos, patient or provider information, or the Parties’ relationship in marketing, customer lists, case studies, demonstrations, social media, or other publicity without HMT’s prior written consent.
12. No Obligation; No Warranty
Nothing requires HMT to purchase, subscribe to, implement, or continue Recipient’s products or services. HMT may end its evaluation subject to any separate signed agreement. Except as separately agreed, Confidential Information is provided without representation or warranty as to completeness or accuracy.
13. Term and Survival
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and remains in effect during the Parties’ relationship and for five (5) years thereafter. Trade-secret obligations continue while the information remains a trade secret. Obligations concerning PHI, patient information, PII, and legally protected information continue as required by law and any BAA.
14. Remedies
Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause irreparable harm for which money damages are inadequate. HMT may seek injunctive or equitable relief, in addition to other available remedies.
15. Governing Law and Venue
Ohio law governs, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. The Parties consent to jurisdiction and venue in state and federal courts located in Ohio.
16. General Terms
This Agreement is the entire agreement concerning confidentiality of the covered information and supersedes prior discussions on that subject, except agreements imposing additional privacy or security duties. For PHI, the provision affording greater protection controls to the extent permitted by law. Amendments and waivers must be in a writing signed by authorized representatives. Recipient may not assign this Agreement without HMT’s prior written consent, except in a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets where the successor agrees in writing to be bound. Invalid provisions are severed without affecting the remainder. Counterparts and electronic signatures are valid.
