## Addendum to Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement

This Addendum to the Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement dated __________________, 2026 (the "NDA") is entered into by HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc. ("HMT") and Arthur Dobelis and Scott Binger, a general partnership trading as Dashbud ("Dashbud").

### 1. Mutual Confidentiality

The NDA is amended to apply reciprocally. All references to "Recipient" shall apply to either Party when receiving Confidential Information from the other. All references to "HMT" as the disclosing Party shall apply equally to Dashbud when Dashbud is the disclosing Party. For the avoidance of doubt:

(a) The definition of "Confidential Information" in Section 2 applies to non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, including Dashbud's product architecture, pricing, business plans, technical specifications, roadmap, and implementation details;

(b) The obligations in Section 3 (Recipient Obligations) bind each Party when receiving the other's Confidential Information;

(c) The restrictions in Section 4 (Artificial Intelligence and Model Training) apply mutually;

(d) The no-publicity restriction in Section 11 applies mutually — neither Party shall use the other's name, trademarks, or the Parties' relationship in marketing or publicity without prior written consent; and

(e) The exclusions in Section 8, the required-disclosure provision in Section 9, and the return-and-destruction provision in Section 10 apply to both Parties symmetrically.

Nothing in this Addendum reduces the protections afforded to HMT's Confidential Information, PHI, or patient information under the NDA or the Business Associate Agreement.

### 2. Limited Publicity Consent

Notwithstanding Section 11 of the NDA, upon HMT's first payment to Dashbud under a paid subscription or service agreement, HMT consents to Dashbud identifying HMT as a Dashbud customer in marketing materials, customer lists, and website content. This consent does not extend to the use of HMT's logos, trademarks, patient or provider information, case study details, or quotations attributed to HMT personnel, each of which continues to require HMT's prior written consent. This consent does not apply during any free trial, evaluation, or unpaid period. HMT may revoke this consent upon thirty (30) days' written notice, after which Dashbud shall remove HMT's name from future materials within a commercially reasonable time.

### 3. Entity Assignment

The Parties acknowledge that Dashbud is currently a general partnership. Upon formation of a successor corporate entity that will carry on Dashbud's business, Dashbud may assign the NDA to the successor entity without HMT's prior consent, provided that:

(a) The successor entity assumes all obligations under the NDA in a writing delivered to HMT;

(b) Dashbud notifies HMT of the assignment within ten (10) business days, including the successor entity's legal name, state of formation, and principal address; and

(c) The assignment does not reduce the protections afforded to HMT under the NDA.

This Section supersedes the assignment restriction in Section 16 of the NDA solely with respect to assignment to Dashbud's successor entity as described herein.

## Addendum to Business Associate Agreement

This Addendum to the Business Associate Agreement dated __________________, 2026 (the "BAA") is entered into by HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc. ("Covered Entity") and Arthur Dobelis and Scott Binger, a general partnership trading as Dashbud ("Business Associate").

### 1. Limitation of Liability

Section 13 (Costs and Cooperation) of the BAA is supplemented as follows:

**(a) Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for loss of profits, revenue, data, or business opportunity, arising out of or related to this BAA or the Underlying Agreement, regardless of the theory of liability (contract, tort, strict liability, or otherwise) and even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**(b) General Cap.** Except as set forth in subsection (c), Business Associate's aggregate liability for all claims arising out of or related to this BAA and the Underlying Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Covered Entity to Business Associate under the Underlying Agreement during the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, excluding any fees for professional services, consulting, or custom development (the "General Cap").

**(c) Elevated Cap.** Business Associate's aggregate liability for claims arising out of (i) breach of its confidentiality or privacy obligations under this BAA or the NDA, (ii) unauthorized use or disclosure of PHI or Confidential Information, or (iii) breach of Section 3 (No AI or Generalized Model Training) shall not exceed ______________________ dollars ($__________) (the "Elevated Cap").


### 2. Entity Assignment

The Parties acknowledge that Business Associate is currently a general partnership. Upon formation of a successor corporate entity that will carry on Business Associate's business, Business Associate may assign the BAA to the successor entity without Covered Entity's prior consent, provided that:

(a) The successor entity assumes all obligations under the BAA in a writing delivered to Covered Entity;

(b) Business Associate notifies Covered Entity of the assignment within ten (10) business days, including the successor entity's legal name, state of formation, and principal address;

(c) The successor entity agrees to comply with all safeguard, security, and privacy obligations under the BAA and the HIPAA Rules; and

(d) The assignment does not reduce the protections afforded to Covered Entity or to PHI under the BAA.

This Section supersedes the assignment restriction in Section 15 of the BAA solely with respect to assignment to Business Associate's successor entity as described herein.
