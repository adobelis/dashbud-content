**Effective Date:** __________________, 2026

## 2. Services

Provider agrees to provide Customer access to the Dashbud platform ("Services"), a cloud-based analytics and reporting solution that enables users to interact with their business data using natural language queries and dashboard tools.

Customer understands and acknowledges that:

- Dashbud connects to Customer-authorized data sources.
- Dashbud uses AI-assisted query generation functionality. AI-generated outputs, including SQL queries and visualizations, are informational and should be independently verified by Customer before use in business decisions.
- Row-level and high-cardinality Customer data is not transmitted to third-party AI services. Low-cardinality reference values (such as category names or status codes) may be shared with AI services only with Authorized User confirmation. Query results are returned directly to the Authorized User's session.
- Customer data accessed via live connectors remains within Customer-controlled systems. Imported data (such as uploaded files) is stored in Provider's hosted environment and protected under this Agreement.
- Provider does not use Customer data to train, retrain, fine-tune, or develop AI models, algorithms, or generalized datasets for Provider or any third party.

## 4. Term and Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months ("Initial Term").

Following the Initial Term, the Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

Provider may adjust the per-user rate upon renewal with at least sixty (60) days' written notice before the start of a renewal term.

## 5. Trial Period

If the Parties agree to a trial or evaluation period before the Initial Term, Provider shall provide access to the Services at no charge for the agreed period. Either Party may end the trial at any time. The trial converts to a paid subscription upon Customer's first payment. During any trial period, this Agreement governs Customer's use of the Services, but no fees are due.

- Fees are non-refundable once paid.

In addition to the subscription Services described in this Agreement, Customer may engage Provider to perform professional services, including but not limited to consulting, implementation support, data integration, custom reporting, dashboard development, training, and related technical services ("Professional Services").

Professional Services shall be scoped and priced by mutual written agreement. Unless otherwise agreed:

- Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to perform requested Professional Services in a timely manner, subject to Provider availability and Customer responsiveness.
- Customer acknowledges that timelines for Professional Services may depend upon Customer responsiveness, system access, and availability of required information.
- Professional Services are separate from the SaaS subscription Services and may be modified or terminated independently upon written agreement of the parties.

Customer agrees to:

- Maintain the confidentiality of user credentials.

- Ensure it has the rights and permissions necessary for any data connected to Dashbud.

## 9. Data Security and Privacy

Provider will implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Customer data, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and audit logging.
