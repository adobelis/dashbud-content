BUSINESS ASSOCIATE AGREEMENT
HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc. and Dashbud
This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is entered into as of __________________, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between HMT Dermatology Associates, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("Covered Entity"), and Dashbud ______________________________, a ______________________________ ("Business Associate"). This BAA supplements any services, trial, evaluation, subscription, data-processing, confidentiality, or other agreement between the Parties (collectively, the "Underlying Agreement").
1. Definitions
Capitalized terms not otherwise defined have the meanings assigned in the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act, and their implementing Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164 (collectively, the "HIPAA Rules"). "PHI" includes Electronic Protected Health Information ("ePHI"). "Business Associate" and "Covered Entity" have the meanings in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and refer to the Parties identified above.
2. Scope and Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to evaluate, configure, test, support, and provide the Dashbud software and related analytics, reporting, automation, and services for Covered Entity, as described in the Underlying Agreement; as expressly directed in writing by Covered Entity; or as Required by Law. Business Associate shall:
Not use or further disclose PHI except as permitted by this BAA or Required by Law;
Limit uses, disclosures, and requests to the Minimum Necessary and follow Covered Entity’s communicated minimum-necessary policies;
Not use or disclose PHI in a manner that would violate 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart E, if done by Covered Entity;
Not sell PHI, use PHI for marketing, or receive remuneration for PHI except as expressly permitted by HIPAA and authorized in writing by Covered Entity; and
Not use PHI for Business Associate’s independent product development, benchmarking across customers, advertising, commercialization, or any purpose unrelated to services for Covered Entity.
Business Associate may use PHI for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities only when the use is permitted by this BAA. It may disclose PHI for those purposes only if Required by Law or after obtaining reasonable written assurances that the recipient will keep it confidential, use it only for the stated purpose or as Required by Law, and notify Business Associate of any breach of confidentiality. No data-aggregation service is authorized unless Covered Entity separately approves it in writing.
3. No AI or Generalized Model Training
Unless Covered Entity expressly authorizes it in a separate signed writing, Business Associate shall not use PHI or data derived from PHI to train, retrain, fine-tune, validate, improve, or develop any artificial intelligence model, machine-learning model, large language model, algorithm, generalized dataset, or product for Business Associate or any third party. Business Associate shall not place PHI or derived data into any public, shared, multi-customer, or generally available model or training dataset. Processing within the Dashbud service must be limited to providing services specifically for Covered Entity.
4. Safeguards and Security Rule Compliance
Business Associate shall use appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent use or disclosure of PHI other than as permitted by this BAA and shall comply with 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C, with respect to ePHI. Safeguards shall include, as applicable, access controls based on job responsibilities, unique user identification, authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, secure configuration, vulnerability and patch management, workforce training, contingency planning, secure disposal, and documented risk analysis and risk management. Business Associate shall maintain policies and records reasonably sufficient to demonstrate compliance and shall provide relevant evidence of safeguards to Covered Entity upon reasonable request, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.
5. Reporting Security Incidents and Breaches
Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not permitted by this BAA, any Breach of Unsecured PHI, and any Security Incident of which it becomes aware. Initial notice must be made without unreasonable delay and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after discovery or reasonable suspicion. Routine unsuccessful security events that do not result in unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction need not be individually reported, but Business Associate shall provide summaries upon reasonable request.
Business Associate shall promptly investigate, mitigate harmful effects, preserve evidence, and cooperate with Covered Entity. Without unreasonable delay, it shall provide the information reasonably available for Covered Entity’s compliance with 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.400–414, including: the nature and date of the incident and discovery; categories and approximate number of affected individuals and records; identification of each affected individual when known; types of PHI involved; corrective and mitigation actions; and a knowledgeable contact. Business Associate shall supplement its report as information becomes available and shall not notify individuals, regulators, media, or others on Covered Entity’s behalf unless Covered Entity directs or authorizes it in writing, except as Required by Law.
6. Subcontractors
Before allowing a Subcontractor to create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI, Business Associate shall enter into a written agreement requiring the Subcontractor to comply with the same restrictions, conditions, safeguards, and Security Rule duties that apply to Business Associate. Business Associate remains responsible for its Subcontractors’ performance. Upon request, Business Associate shall identify Subcontractors that handle Covered Entity PHI and the countries or regions in which PHI is stored or processed. Business Associate shall not materially change PHI-hosting locations or add a Subcontractor with access to PHI without prior written notice to Covered Entity and an opportunity for Covered Entity to object on reasonable privacy or security grounds.
7. Individual Rights and Covered Entity Requests
To enable Covered Entity to meet its obligations, Business Associate shall:
Make PHI in a Designated Record Set available to Covered Entity in the form, format, and time reasonably requested for compliance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.524;
Make amendments to PHI as directed or agreed by Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526;
Maintain and make available information needed for an accounting of disclosures under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528; and
Forward to Covered Entity within three (3) business days any request, complaint, or inquiry received directly from an Individual concerning Covered Entity PHI, unless the Parties have agreed in writing that Business Associate will respond.
Business Associate shall fulfill Covered Entity requests under this Section within ten (10) business days unless Covered Entity specifies a shorter reasonable period required for legal compliance.
8. Privacy Rule Functions and Government Access
To the extent Business Associate performs an obligation of Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart E, it shall comply with the requirements applicable to Covered Entity in performing that obligation. Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services for determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.
9. Covered Entity Responsibilities
Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of limitations in its Notice of Privacy Practices, changes or revocations of an Individual’s permission, and restrictions under 45 C.F.R. § 164.522, in each case to the extent they may affect Business Associate’s use or disclosure. Covered Entity shall not request a use or disclosure that would be impermissible under the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity, except to the extent a permitted Business Associate management or administration use is expressly included in this BAA.
10. Data Ownership, Access, and Portability
As between the Parties, Covered Entity retains all rights in its PHI and data. Business Associate acquires no ownership interest in PHI by processing, analyzing, organizing, transforming, or storing it. Business Associate shall not withhold PHI because of a payment dispute. On reasonable request and at termination, Business Associate shall provide Covered Entity a complete, usable export of Covered Entity PHI in a mutually agreed, commonly readable format.
11. Term and Termination
This BAA begins on the Effective Date and continues while Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI for Covered Entity. Covered Entity may terminate this BAA and the affected services immediately upon determining that Business Associate violated a material term. At Covered Entity’s discretion, it may first provide a reasonable opportunity to cure if cure is feasible and delay will not materially increase privacy or security risk.
12. Return or Destruction
Upon termination for any reason, Business Associate shall return or, at Covered Entity’s direction, securely destroy all PHI received from Covered Entity or created, maintained, or received for Covered Entity, including PHI held by Subcontractors, and retain no copies. It shall provide written certification upon request. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall explain the basis in writing, retain only the PHI for which return or destruction is infeasible, continue all protections, limit further use and disclosure to the purpose making return or destruction infeasible, and return or destroy the PHI when feasible. PHI in routine backups may remain until overwritten under documented secure retention procedures, must remain protected, and may not be restored or used except for legitimate disaster recovery. These duties survive termination.
13. Costs and Cooperation
To the extent an incident results from Business Associate’s or its Subcontractor’s violation of this BAA or applicable law, Business Associate shall reimburse Covered Entity for reasonable, documented, legally required costs of investigation, mitigation, notification, credit or identity monitoring when appropriate, regulatory response, and restoration. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith regarding response strategy and communications.
14. Order of Precedence
This BAA controls over conflicting terms in the Underlying Agreement concerning PHI. If the separate Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement or another signed agreement gives PHI greater protection, the more protective provision controls to the extent permitted by law.
15. General Terms
References to the HIPAA Rules mean those provisions as amended. The Parties shall amend this BAA as necessary for legal compliance. Ambiguities shall be interpreted to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. Neither Party may assign this BAA except with a permitted assignment of the Underlying Agreement and written assumption by the successor. Ohio law governs issues not preempted by federal law, and the Parties consent to jurisdiction and venue in state and federal courts in Ohio. This BAA may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature. It constitutes the entire agreement on its subject matter, and amendments must be in a writing signed by both Parties.
