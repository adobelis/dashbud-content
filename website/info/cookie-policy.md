# Cookie Policy

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how **Dashbud** (“we”, “our”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and platform.
It describes what these technologies are, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser.
They allow websites to remember information about your visit, such as login status, preferences, or analytics data.

We also use similar technologies (e.g., localStorage) where needed for functionality.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We group cookies into the same categories you see in our consent banner:

- **Strictly Necessary**
Required for core site functions (e.g., security, session management). They are always enabled.

- **Analytics**
Help us understand how visitors use Dashbud so we can improve performance and usability.
For example: Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager).

- **Ads & Tracking**
Used to measure ad performance or remarketing campaigns (e.g., Google Ads, LinkedIn).
We don’t currently run large-scale ad tracking, but we keep this category for future use.



We don’t sell your data or use third-party advertising networks without consent.

## 4. Your Choices

When you first visit Dashbud, you’ll see a banner allowing you to:

- **Accept all cookies**

- **Reject non-essential cookies**

- **Manage preferences** (choose Analytics and/or Ads individually)



You can update your preferences anytime by selecting **“Update cookie preferences”** in our site footer.
Your choices are remembered in your browser until you clear them or reset via that link.

## 5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies entirely.
If you do so, essential site functions may stop working (e.g., login sessions).

See browser help pages for instructions:

- Chrome

- Firefox

- Safari

- Edge

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices.
Material updates will be highlighted in the banner or via a notice on this page.
