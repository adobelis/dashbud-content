# AI Doesn't Reduce Work, It Intensifies It — HBR

## Summary (abstract only; article paywalled)

New research: AI tools don't reduce work, they consistently intensify it. Employees worked faster, took on a broader scope of tasks, and extended work into more hours, often unasked. Short-term productivity surge, then workload creep, cognitive fatigue, burnout, weaker decisions, lower quality, turnover. Prescription: an "AI practice," norms around AI use including intentional pauses, sequencing work, and human grounding.
