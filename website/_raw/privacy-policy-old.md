Dashbud -- Privacy Policy
1. Overview
Evolytix, Inc. (“Evolytix”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and your data. This Privacy Policy describes how Dashbud collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our platform. It also explains what we do not do — most importantly, that Dashbud does not ingest or store any of your underlying data.

2. What We Collect
When you use Dashbud, we collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services. This includes:

User Account Information: Name, email address, organization name, and authentication credentials.
Connection Metadata: Information about the type of database you connect (e.g., SQL Server, Snowflake), including the schema, table names, column names, and data types.
Usage Logs: Activity logs related to feature usage, error reporting, and system diagnostics (all anonymized or pseudonymized wherever possible).
3. What We Do Not Collect or Store
This is important:

Dashbud does NOT ingest or store your actual business data (i.e., the records, values, or contents in your database tables).

Our platform only accesses database structure and metadata, such as:

Table names
Column names
Column types
Relationships between tables (e.g., foreign keys)
This metadata allows Dashbud to intelligently construct SQL queries based on your natural language questions — without ever exposing the underlying data to our systems or any third-party AI models.

You maintain full control of your data. Dashbud acts as a query-generation layer on top of your existing data infrastructure.

4. How We Use the Information
We use the information we collect strictly to:

Authenticate users and provide access to the Dashbud platform
Generate AI-assisted queries using your schema metadata
Improve platform performance and reliability
Provide support and troubleshoot issues
Enforce our terms and protect against abuse
We do not use your information for advertising, resell it to third parties, or allow any third-party AI models (including LLMs) to access your schema or data.

5. Security and Data Access
We implement strong technical and organizational security measures, including:

Role-based access control
Encrypted connections (TLS/SSL) for all data in transit
Logging and monitoring of access activity
Only authorized personnel may access customer-specific metadata, and only for purposes directly related to support or diagnostics.

6. Third-Party Services
Dashbud does not share any customer schema or metadata with external third-party services unless explicitly authorized by you, the customer. No analytics, ad platforms, or AI APIs process your data behind the scenes.

7. Data Retention
We retain user account data only for as long as your account remains active.

Schema metadata is retained temporarily to allow query generation but is never stored permanently unless you choose to save a dashboard or report.

You may request deletion of your account or any retained metadata at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights
You have the right to:

Request access to your user profile data
Request correction or deletion of your account information
Withdraw consent for optional data processing (e.g., usage analytics)
File a complaint with a regulatory authority, if applicable in your jurisdiction
9. Children’s Privacy
Dashbud is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date. Material changes will be communicated via email or dashboard notification.

11. Contact Us
For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@mail.getdashbud.com
