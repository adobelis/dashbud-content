An enlightened approach to your data.

Give Dashbud your spreadsheets, your giant ERP exports, your SaaS APIs, the awful mess of data in your files. Dashbud gives you the ability to produce the answers you need.

Why Dashbud?

We built Dashbud to bridge the gap between the promise of business data analysis and the reality of what most people can get from their data. Dashbud provides:
* A flexible, organized, scalable approach to connecting, storing, and accessing data. 
* A user-friendly, AI-enabled interface that gives anyone the power to answer sophisticated data questions and generate professional-quality charts, tables, dashboards, and reports.
* An interface that scales from one user to a whole organization, with thoughtful attention to data security and governance.
* An AI-based system that avoids the serious pitfalls and perils of AI-based workflows and DIY reporting.

How does it work? (diagram?)
* Import files or connect data sources: spreadsheets, databases and warehoses, APIs, and system exports. 
* Dashbud's data modeling agent learns your data's structure and business context. 
* **Ask questions in plain language.** Dashbud's data analyst agent answers your questions and generates tables, charts, and reports for you.
* Create reports and dashboards for your own use; share them with your team and stakeholders. 

Who is it great for? 
* Individuals: organize your data better, combine data from files, cloud resources like SaaS APIs, and external data stores. 
* Small businesses: manage data access so everyone gets the data they need, create shared reporting and put the power to answer questions in everyone's hands. Keep sensitive data private and secure. Top use cases include helathcare regulated businesses. 
* Mid-tier companies: access data from diverse data sources such as data lakes, legacy systems (ERP, CRM, EMR, etc.), and SaaS platforms. Smart governance features allow you to provide access with confidence.
* Enterprise: scale access and governance to the enterprise, putting power in the hands of users; give time back to your data team to answer the hard questions, not simple reporting requests. **Deploy Dashbud in your own cloud for compliance.** 
* Everyone: give AI agents governed access through Dashbud's analysis API.
