# Dashbud Pricing

Start with a free trial and scale as your use grows.


### Starter

For small businesses and startups.

- One individual license
- Up to 5 active workspaces
- Teams up to 3 people
- Unlimited access to AI data builder and conversations
- Unlimited customizable charts and graphs
- Unlimited custom dashboards

### Scale

For larger teams, agencies, and consultants.

- One individual license
- All Starter features
- Up to 20 active workspaces to start
- Teams up to 10 people

### Enterprise

For organizations with custom requirements and enterprise-grade needs.

- All Scale features
- Unlimited active workspaces
- Unlimited team members
- Additional APIs and integrations on demand
