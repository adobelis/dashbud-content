## Option A — Lead with the problem

I've spent a lot of my career as the person who ends up pulling data and building reports — because I could. Export from the ERP, clean it up, build the pivot table, make the chart, send it around. Repeat next month.

That workflow is everywhere. Mid-market companies running on legacy systems — ERPs, medical records platforms, operational databases — have enormous amounts of data, but getting useful reporting out of it still means someone with the skills and the patience to wrangle it manually.

We built Dashbud to fix that. It's an AI-powered analytics platform that connects to your existing data sources, helps you define your business rules, and lets your team ask questions and build reports conversationally. No SQL, no data team, no six-month implementation.

The AI in Dashbud doesn't work the way most people expect. It never sees your raw data. It works from the structure and business context — metadata, relationships, rules — and generates accurate, auditable queries against a governed data source. Privacy is in the architecture, not the fine print.

We're live and working with companies now. If your organization is data-rich but analytics-poor, I'd love to talk.
